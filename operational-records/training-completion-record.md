## Purpose

This record documents training programme completion data for Emyzer Nexus (including Emyzer Technology and Veridian AI staff) for Q1 2025. It is produced jointly by Human Resources and the GRC team to support compliance monitoring, regulatory reporting (including AI Act operator training requirements [VERIFY]), and internal audit.

This record references training obligations set out in the following policies:
- **Security Awareness and Training Policy — Emyzer Technology (Phase 1)** — applicable to all Emyzer Technology employees
- **Security Awareness and Training Policy — Emyzer Nexus (Phase 2, Tier 2)** — applicable to all Emyzer Nexus group staff including Veridian AI personnel following the Q4 2024 acquisition

This record also documents compliance with the AI Governance Policy requirement that all designated operators of High-Risk AI systems (PCM-001 and CRT-001) complete mandatory operator training before system access is granted and annually thereafter. Access control system integration ensures that system access cannot be provisioned until the relevant training completion record is confirmed.

Training data is drawn from the organisation's Learning Management System (LMS — Workday Learning). Completion rates reflect the status as at the last LMS extract: **31 March 2025, 23:59 UTC**.

## Section 5: Phishing Simulation Results — Q1 2025

The Q1 2025 phishing simulation was conducted during the week of **3–7 March 2025** using the organisation's email security and awareness platform.

**Simulation summary:**

| Metric                                     | Q1 2025   | Q4 2024 (comparison) | Target       |
|--------------------------------------------|-----------|----------------------|--------------|
| Total recipients                           | 4,400     | 4,350                | All active mailboxes |
| Emails delivered successfully              | 4,387     | 4,341                | —            |
| Links clicked (click rate)                 | 212 (4.8%) | 266 (6.1%)          | <8.0%        |
| Phishing reported (report rate)            | 1,360 (31.0%) | 1,087 (25.0%)     | >25%         |
| Credentials entered (of those who clicked) | 44 (20.8% of clickers) | 71 (26.7%)    | 0%           |
| Departments above 8% click threshold      | 1 (Customer Success: 8.2%) | 2 (Customer Support 9.4%, Sales 8.7%) | 0 |

**Trend:** Improving. Click rate has declined from 6.1% in Q4 2024 to 4.8% in Q1 2025, reflecting continued effectiveness of the quarterly simulation programme and the uplift from the December 2024 post-incident phishing awareness campaign following INC-2024-041 (spear-phishing attempt against Finance department — no compromise).

**Remedial training:**
The Customer Success department exceeded the 8% click-rate trigger threshold. Per policy, all Customer Success staff who clicked the simulation link (31 individuals) have been automatically assigned the **Targeted Phishing Awareness remedial module** in the LMS, due by 2025-04-30. The Customer Success Head of Department has been notified. GRC to confirm completions in the Q2 training record.

**Simulation design note:** The Q1 simulation used a credential-harvesting template mimicking a Workday password reset notification — a scenario with elevated realism given Workday is the active HRIS platform. This was approved by the CISO as an advanced simulation scenario consistent with the organisation's simulated threat maturity progression.
