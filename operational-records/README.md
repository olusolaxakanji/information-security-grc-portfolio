## About This Folder

This folder contains sample operational records produced through the routine operation of the GRC programme. These records constitute the evidence layer of the control framework: they are the artefacts auditors inspect to confirm that documented controls are operating as described, not merely as written.

Each record maps to one or more controls in the [controls library](https://github.com/olusolaxakanji/information-security-grc-portfolio/tree/main/controls) and demonstrates what real operational evidence looks like for common GRC assurance activities.

## How Operational Records Relate to Controls

The GRC control framework operates at three levels:

1. **Policy**: the *requirement* (e.g., the Access Control Policy mandates quarterly access reviews)
2. **Control**: the *procedure* (e.g., ET-CTRL-001 defines how the quarterly review is conducted, by whom, and what evidence is produced)
3. **Operational record**: the *evidence* (e.g., the access review attestation record proves that Q1 2025's review was conducted, completed, and attested)

Without the operational record, a control is a documented intention. The operational record is what turns the intention into demonstrable assurance.

---

## Evidence Standards Applied

All records in this folder are prepared to the following standards:

- Records are produced within the timeframe specified in the governing control
- Each record identifies the system owner, reviewer, and approver by role
- Exception findings are documented with risk ratings, remediation actions, and accountable owners
- Sign-off attestations confirm review completion
- Records are retained in the GRC platform for the period specified in the Evidence Index of the governing control
